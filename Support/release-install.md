### Install

1. Download **PixelShift-*.zip** below and open it.
2. Drag **PixelShift** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. If macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software, click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to PixelShift. You only need to do this once.

Already have PixelShift? Choose **PixelShift → Check for Updates…**. Requires macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
